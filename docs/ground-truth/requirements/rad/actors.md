# System Actors

Source: RAD v1.0.0, §§ 3.1–3.1.1, pp. 10–12.

## Utente

General actor representing registered platform users.

- Register and authenticate.
- View and update personal information.
- Delete their account.
- Maintain pseudonymity during listening sessions.

## Caller

Person aged 14 or above seeking emotional support.

- Submit listening requests with thematic categories and
  an optional description.
- Cancel requests before acceptance.
- Communicate with the assigned Helper via text or
  unrecorded audio calls.
- Provide feedback.

## Caller Minorenne

Caller aged 14–17.

- Access through parental authorization or Safe Access Path.

## Helper

Trained and accredited volunteer affiliated with a partner
organization, providing non-professional emotional support.

- Manage availability.
- Accept or reject listening requests.
- Communicate with the Caller.
- Trigger Safe Escalation.

## Supervisor

Specifically qualified volunteer supporting Helpers in
platform procedures, reports and escalations.

- Verify and assign Helper qualifications.
- Provide real-time support during escalation.
- Manage handover to competent authorities or emergency services.

## Generalization

- Caller specializes Utente.
- Helper specializes Utente.
- Supervisor specializes Utente.
- Caller Minorenne specializes Caller.
