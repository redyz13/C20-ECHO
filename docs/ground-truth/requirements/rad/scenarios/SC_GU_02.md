# SC_GU_02 — Helper Qualification Assignment or Update

Source: RAD v1.0.0, § 3.4.1.5, p. 39.
Actors: Supervisor, Helper.

| Step | Actor | System |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Supervisor accesses their restricted area and opens "Helper Management". | Retrieves and displays the list of registered Helpers. |
| 2 | Selects a Helper after receiving documentation confirming completion of a new internal training module. | Retrieves the Helper's data and displays the profile with currently active qualifications. |
| 3 | Selects "Edit qualifications", chooses the relevant new certification and confirms the update. | Validates the operation, updates the Helper's qualifications, notifies the Helper and displays confirmation to the Supervisor. |
| 4 | Reviews the confirmation and closes the Helper profile. | Closes the details screen and returns to the Helper list. |
