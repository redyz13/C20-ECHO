# SC_GA_02 — Minor Access with Parental Authorization

Source: RAD v1.0.0, § 3.4.1.1, p. 27.
Actors: Caller Minorenne.

| Step | Actor | System |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A 16-year-old Caller experiencing emotional distress accesses E.C.H.O. | Displays the home page and user information notice. |
| 2 | Reads the notice and completes the age questionnaire. | Identifies the Caller as a minor (14–17) and redirects them to the SPID service for minors. |
| 3 | Enters SPID credentials. The parents authorize access. | Receives Identity Provider data and positive parental authorization, then confirms successful authorization. |
| 4 | Receives authorization confirmation. | Redirects the Caller to their personal area and grants access to the service. |
