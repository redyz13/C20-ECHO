# SC_GA_03 — Access through Safe Access Path

Source: RAD v1.0.0, § 3.4.1.1, p. 28.
Actors: Caller Minorenne.

| Step | Actor | System |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A 15-year-old Caller who feels unable to confide in their family accesses E.C.H.O. for emotional support. | Displays the home page and user information notice. |
| 2 | Reads the notice, completes the age questionnaire and indicates that involving their parents would not feel safe. | Identifies the Caller as a minor (14–17) and grants guest access because parental involvement is not possible. |
| 3 | Receives guest access confirmation. | Redirects the Caller to the guest personal area, enabling the Safe Access Path. |
