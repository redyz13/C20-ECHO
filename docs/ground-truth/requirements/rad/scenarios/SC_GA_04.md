# SC_GA_04 — Registration Denied to Users Under 14

Source: RAD v1.0.0, § 3.4.1.1, p. 29.
Actors: Utente (13 years old).

| Step | Actor | System |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A 13-year-old user experiencing emotional distress accesses E.C.H.O. | Displays the home page, user information notice and questionnaire. |
| 2 | Reads the notice and completes the age questionnaire. | Receives Identity Provider data, identifies the user as under 14, informs them of the minimum age requirement and displays alternative informational resources. |
| 3 | Reads the message, notes the alternative resources and leaves the platform. | — |
