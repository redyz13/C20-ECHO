# System Context

Source: RAD v1.0.0, §§ 1.1–1.3, 1.6, 2.1, 3.1.

## System Objective

E.C.H.O. is a greenfield platform providing free, secure,
pseudonymous and non-professional emotional support to
people aged 14 and above through trained and accredited
volunteers.

The service is supported by the Public Administration and
Third Sector organizations. It does not provide therapy,
diagnosis or clinical counselling, nor replace healthcare,
social or emergency services.

## System Scope

- User registration and authentication.
- Parental authorization and Safe Access Path for minors.
- Deterministic matching based on availability and eligibility.
- Real-time text chat and WebRTC audio calls.
- Session supervision and Safe Escalation.
- Session termination and anonymous feedback.

## Objectives and Success Criteria

- Accessible and confidential listening and guidance.
- Protection of minors and safe management of risk situations.
- Operational support for Helper and Supervisor roles.
- Delivery by December 2026.
- Implementation of all high-priority functional requirements.
- Correct operation of safeguarding and escalation procedures.
- Pseudonymous interactions without retaining communication content.
